Frequently Asked Questions

What does S.H.O.E.S factory do?

We customize classic sneakers & transform them into works of art, that resonate with you.

Can I request my own design?

Yes! You can either book an existing design from our Collection, or work with us for a completely custom 1-of-1 pair.

Will I have to provide the shoe?

If you have a clean pair which can be shipped to our Studio in New Delhi, we can use it for your custom! If not, then we will source the shoe for you.

Are your shoes weather resistant?

Each sneaker is carefully handcrafted by our Customization Experts, using top of line tools, techniques and materials to ensure that your sneaker stands the test of time and weather.

Can I return my order?

All sneakers are custom made to your specific requests, hence cannot be exchanged or returned.
